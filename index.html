THE PROBLEM

Healthcare can feel disconnected.

SmartCare is designed around the small problems that make a hospital visit harder than it needs to be.

01

Long waiting

See appointment and queue information before you reach the consultation room.

02

Scattered records

Keep visits, reports, prescriptions and allergies together in one patient profile.

03

Slow prioritization

A simple triage step helps staff identify urgent cases earlier.

04

Missed follow-ups

Keep the next appointment, medicine and check-up visible after every visit.
